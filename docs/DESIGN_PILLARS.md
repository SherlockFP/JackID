# JackID: Design Pillars (director, 2026-10-15)

Read this before adding any feature. Every wave's critic checks new work against it.
The client's rule: take ideas from CloverPit, Balatro, Peglin and Welcome to the Game, but **no soup**. A borrowed mechanic is
re-expressed through JackID's own fiction, or it is cut.

## The one-line identity
**By night you gamble in the House to pay the mob. By dawn you go home to a family that is waiting, and to a door that someone may knock on.**

## Pillars
1. **Debt is the clock.** The quota is the mob's deadline, not an abstract target. Every Night is a payday. Pressure is told by
   people: the mob texts, a knock comes at the door, a goon waits in the hallway. If you miss, the mob comes in.
2. **Home is the luck source, the House is the money source.** Money is won at the machines. Luck (blessings, rest, calm, the
   spouse's faith) is earned at home. A good night at home makes tomorrow's table kinder. A bad night (no sleep, a fight, a
   visit from the mob) makes it colder.
3. **Every choice has a face.** A choice comes from someone: the House (risk and greed), the Family (safety and warmth), or the
   Mob (deals with a price). Night Cards, phone replies and door decisions are the same idea seen from three doors.
4. **Juice is the reward language.** Every win is felt before it is read: anticipation, a count-up, sound pitched to the size,
   light, and the hands reacting. Bigger wins escalate on one shared ladder (WIN, BIG WIN, HUGE, JACKPOT), so the player learns
   it once. Near misses are framed. Losses are quick and quiet.
5. **Dread is earned, not random.** Horror comes from the player's own state: debt behind schedule, mob heat, lights on, noise at
   home. Calm mode always mutes scares.
6. **PSX honesty.** Low poly, a 15-bit palette, dither and vertex snap. Effects are chunky: pixel particles, hard flashes,
   stepped count-ups. No smooth modern bloom soup.
7. **One mechanic, many readings.** Before adding a new system, extend an existing one: charms, Night Cards, stamps, overshoot,
   House Mood, the bank, tickets. A new currency or meter needs the director's sign-off.

## How each borrowed idea lands in JackID
| Source | Idea | JackID reading |
|---|---|---|
| Balatro | chips × mult escalation, score count-up | **Heat**: a winning streak warms a machine (neon brightens, multiplier climbs, pitch rises); pushed too far, it overheats. The Dawn ledger counts up like a score. |
| CloverPit | deadline, charms, a run that snowballs | The quota is the **mob's deadline**. Charms are keepsakes with an origin: the spouse's token, a pawned watch, a "gift" from the mob. |
| Peglin | bounce physics, a combo per bounce | Pachinko and plinko **bounce combos**. Each peg ticks a combo counter and raises the pitch; the last bounce is a framed near miss. |
| Welcome to the Game | home at night, door, peephole, footsteps, phone, a threat you must listen for | **Home layer**: elevator up to the apartment, hallway footsteps, peephole, chain, lights off and stay still. The phone carries spouse and mob threads. Missing the quota ends with the mob walking in. |

## Not allowed
- A mechanic with no fiction ("+10 % luck card" that no one gave you).
- A second copy of an existing system (a new shop, a new card type, or a new currency) when an existing one can carry it.
- Names, characters or text from the games above.
- Scares that ignore Calm mode, or horror unrelated to the player's state.
