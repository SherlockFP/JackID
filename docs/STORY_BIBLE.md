# JackID: Story Bible (lead writer, 2026-10-19)

Read with docs/DESIGN_PILLARS.md. This gives THE SECOND NAME (wave 73) one spine. Original IP only: the spirit of gambling-duel anime (mind games, eccentric gamblers, a hidden power structure, cheat and counter-cheat, cold reads, cliffhangers), none of their names, rules, plots or lines.

---

## 1. Logline, themes, tone

**Logline.** Every night a man goes down into the House, a casino built over a wishing well, to pay off a jackpot he doesn't remember winning. Under his name on the card is a second name. Every dawn he goes home to the woman it belongs to.

**Themes.** Love is a debt someone else pays. The House runs on distrust, and the one thing it cannot price is a kept word. The House takes faces, so you learn to read what is left: eyes, hands, numbers. Everyone answers one question: what would you put on the table for someone else? Mira gave her name. Ada keeps a secret. The Collector came back. You stake yourself.

**Tone.** Dark, with dry humour: the House is polite, the Collector says thank you, the Compere is a ham, a receipt lists "one hand (left)". Home is the opposite: sincere and quiet. The House can be funny. The kitchen never is.

## 2. The world

**The Well (Kuyu).** An old wishing well under the city. Throw a coin, make a wish. It almost never answers. When it does, that is a jackpot. It grants on credit.

**The House (Kasa).** A casino built on the Well to collect what the Well is owed. It is a casino because that is the only place where people feed a hole in the ground every night and say thank you. Seven floors down (B7 to B1), then the Ground. Each paid Night the lift takes you one floor up. Night 8 is the street door.

**The JackID card.** The receipt. Front: JACKPOT and a date. Back: your name and the debt, and under it, in small print, the second name. The card is always a little warm.

**Who owns the card.** The House keeps the card and the Collector works the debt. The Three Seats own the House, and the Three Seats owe the Well. Everyone here is somebody's debtor. The bottom of the chain is a hole in the ground.

**The Law of the House** (five rules on the Clerk's plate, then in the journal):
1. A wager said over a card is binding. The Clerk hears it.
2. Anyone may cheat. A cheat proven to the Clerk loses the wager. A wrong accusation pays a fine.
3. Every card has two names: the one who wished, and what they wished for. When the first can't pay, the House takes back the second.
4. The first payment on any card is a name.
5. The lift takes up only the first name. A card burns only when the House loses a clean wager at its own table.

**Ranks.**
- **Guest (Misafir):** holds a card and pays the quota.
- **Chair (Sandalye):** sold part of their name for a table. Chairs play guests for the House and win their name back a piece at a time. None has finished.
- **Still one (Donuk):** a name fully taken. They staff the floor, fill the seats and never blink.
- **The Clerk (Katip):** rules and records. The only thing in the House that owes nothing.
- **The Three Seats (Üç Koltuk):** the Lords of Ash (time), Ledgers (money) and Mirrors (faces), behind the Hollow Door. Owners, and bored.

**Debts paid in other things:**
- name seals (three per card; at zero the card reads BLANK and you go still)
- keepsakes (ring, watch, photo)
- faces (always taken first)
- memories (the House keeps the wish, you keep the debt)
- words (a broken promise is written down as Heat, the Collector's 0-6 meter)

**Why everyone is a silhouette.** The House takes your face first, because a face is how people find you. Anyone written in the Clerk's book shows to a card holder as a black shape. The rim of light is what is left of their own name, in their own colour. The eyes glow because the House never takes eyes: a guest has to see the table. The rims tell you who someone is:

| Rim | Who |
|---|---|
| cold cyan | still ones |
| red | the Collector |
| warm amber | Ada (the only warm light on any figure) |
| white eyes | the House is playing through that person |

Manga portraits show faces because reading a person gives their face back for a moment. That is why faces appear only in duels and close talks. The player sees Ada as a silhouette from the first scene and blames the light ("The light is wrong", intro.1). It is the biggest clue in the game, in plain sight.

## 3. Cast

**YOU, "the Mark" (Keriz).** Gloved hands, a wristwatch, a cheap suit. Silent to others (choices are one-line replies). Inner monologue in duels and at home only. Doesn't know what he wished for. The gloves hide fingertips going dark (N7).

**ADA (spouse).** Dark hair in a loose low knot, a mustard cardigan over a house dress, a beauty mark under the left eye. In 3D: amber rim, soft gold eyes; her portrait is the warmest in the game. Warm and teasing; always opens with paper.
- **Secret:** a year ago she died for one night. You went down and wished her back. She remembers; you don't. She is the second name. Harmony 0 ("Gone to my sister's") means her rim has gone out and she is down with Mira until you pay her back up.
- Arc: N1 teaches you the game for the dishes. N5 plays you for the truth and breaks her word for the first time. N8 asks you to come home first.

**MIRA (Ada's older sister).** Once the House's best Chair: she won by keeping her word in front of liars. She made the first payment on your card with her own name, so she lives in the card: that is why it is warm and why she can only call. Seen once (N8): a man's suit too big for her, cropped hair, violet rim. Wants her sister safe, and to be put down.
- Arc: N2 wrong number. N5 "the Clerk is counting". N8 sits across from you.

**ORRIN PALE, THE COLLECTOR (Tahsildar).** The TALLY rival and the phone Collector, now one man. Grey tailcoat under a wet raincoat, the Manager's Key on a chain, red rim. Polite and exact; signs "O." Game: The Question. Tell: thumb along the card edge. Cheat: nicked high cards. **Wants** his wife back: he paid in full, the lift took only him, and she is still in the Hall, so he works for the House to stay near her.
- Arc: N1-N2 courteous calls. N5 the knock. N6 sits down to play you.

**THE CLERK (Katip).** The first card ever printed, standing up. A paper-white blank face, no rim, no eyes. Rules every wager, writes every name, cannot lie or be paid. Replaces the old Notaries.
- Arc: N3 rules Lucan's match. N7 audits you. N8 burns the card or keeps it.

**THE COMPERE (Sunucu).** The floor's voice. Sequin tailcoat (the only thing that sparkles), corded mic, pink neon rim. Paid in attention: if the floor stops looking, he fades. Can't stop commentating, and that is his tell.
- Arc: N1 welcomes you. From N3 he runs "Ask the Compere". N8 hosts the Burning Wager.

**TAMSIN VEIL, "The Quiet Hand" (Sessiz El).** The courteous smiling cheat. White gloves, silver bob, jade cuffs; her closed eyes glow only when she cheats. Runs the House's crooked tables and apologises before she wins. Tell: "Kusura bakmayın" the turn before. Cheat: a fifth tile in her sleeve. Wants the last piece of her name.
- Arc: N2 hosts the first Crooked Table, delighted when you catch it. Side duel Sealed Wall (N2-N8). Beaten, at N8 she gives you the House's tell: white eyes.

**LUCAN MORROW, "Seventh Chamber" (Yedinci Oda).** The grinning predator. Ringmaster coat, cracked pocket watch, one eye glowing wider than the other, red-orange rim. Builds trust, then cashes it once: four kept words, a break at match point. Tell: checks his watch before every throw he answers (there are no clocks in the House). Cheat: the watch glass mirrors your hand under the table edge. Wants to see a name go out, and a rematch with the only player who beat him: Mira.
- Arc: N3 cold open, he takes a stranger's last seal. N3 boss: Rock, Paper, Promise. N8, front row, he tells you how Mira beat him.

**MIMOSA TEAGUE, "Lost & Found" (Kayıp Eşya).** The cheerful broken girl. Oversized coat, one shoe, a claim tag on her wrist, yellow rim. Runs the Wash's lost and found, lost memories included. Tell: hums while she shuffles. Cheat: a spare key. Wants someone to come back for her.
- Arc: N4 offers a game for "one thing nobody claimed"; Locker Shuffle wins you a hospital wristband. Beaten, at N7 she hands back your memory of the wish.

**VELL, "The Counter" (Sayıcı).** The burnt-out genius who wants to lose. Rain coat, tape reels, grey rim, almost still. Counted every coin in the Well; throws every answer. Tell: drops a coin and leaves it. Cheat: reads the tape ahead. Wants out: a Chair leaves only when a guest calls his cheat.
- Arc: N4-N6 his tapes in the rooms. N7 watches the Audit. N8 The Last Count: call his throw and he rides up, handing you the coin you threw into the Well.

**Cut from the cast:** Kuro (machine Heat already carries "pressure"), the Notaries (now the Clerk), Orrin as a separate rival (now the Collector).

## 5. Mind games

### (a) ROCK, PAPER, PROMISE / TAŞ, KAĞIT, SÖZ

**Hook.** The sisters' game. Mira made it up so little Ada would stop cheating. The House took it over because it prices the one thing the House lacks: a kept word.

**Rules (20 seconds).**
- Rock, paper, scissors. First to 3.
- Before each throw, the caller says what they'll throw: a promise. Callers take turns.
- Keep your word and win: 2 points. Any other win: 1 point.
- Break your word: your card cracks. Three cracks and you lose.

**Depth.** A promise has three answers:
- believe it, and counter;
- doubt it, and throw the same hand (it ties a kept promise and beats the smart break);
- go one step further, and beat the doubter.

Kept wins pay double, so honesty tempts; cracks stop endless breaking. The game is one read: does this person mean it, tonight, at this score? No limited hands: the only resource is your word.

**Beginner: Ada, at home** (N1 tutorial, then once per evening). Cracks cost the dishes, not the match. She keeps her word four times in five, opens with paper, and laughs before she breaks. Stake: dishes, a truth or a kiss. An evening of kept words gives +1 ŞANS tomorrow and Harmony +1 (pillar 2).

**Boss: Lucan (N3).** First to 4. He keeps his early promises and breaks at the winning throw. The watch mirror shows him your hand, so he always answers right.

| Clue | What you see |
|---|---|
| TELL | he checks the watch only before throws he answers |
| TABLE | the glass catches your glove (Gambler's Eye) |
| MATH | Tally Sheet: "He answered five of your throws and won five." |

- **Call:** C, then MIRROR. Rule 2 voids the match in your favour.
- **Counter-cheats:** set the photo keepsake in front of the watch ("I like to look at her when I play"), or switch your hand late. A late switch is a foul if he sees it.
- **Economy:** stake tiers as now (Coins, Pledge, seal). Each kept-word win +1 ŞANS (max 2). Each crack Heat +1: broken words travel. A win pays the boss reward, a Wing Key and his Pledge.

**Finale: the Burning Wager (N8).** First to 5, against the House through Mira. The card burns only if you win with zero cracks.
- **Mira's throws:** she keeps every promise she makes, and when she answers yours she throws to lose.
- **The House's throws:** her violet rim turns white.
  - **A white answer** always counters you. Take the loss, or break your word and win the match without burning the card.
  - **A white call** always breaks her promise, and the crack lands on Mira. The House can afford two. Read the white and throw her promised hand: it beats the break.
- **Earned help:** Tamsin's tip makes the white clear, Lucan's warns you about the clean rule, Mimosa's memory gives +1 Eye. The Compere shouts "The House is thinking!" before white throws, and sometimes when nothing is happening.

### (b) THE QUESTION / SORU (bluff and read)

**Rules (20 seconds).** One card each, 1 to 9, face down. High card wins. Each of you asks one yes-or-no question about the other's card, and the answer may be a lie. Then one bet: raise, see or fold. If the cards are shown and you lied, you pay a fine. First to 5.

**Depth.** What you ask ("above 5?" or "odd?"), how you read the answer, how you answer. A lie pays only if they fold: a bluff with a price.

**Your own tell.** When you lie, your gloved hand trembles on screen, and the rival sees it too. Heat makes it worse, ŞANS steadies it, and "steady hands" food (W77) removes it for a Watch.

**Beginner: "Ask the Compere"** (floor event, N3+). He lies half the time on low cards, and every lie ends with "and that, friends, is the honest truth." Stake: half S.

**Hard: the Collector (N6).** Two cards each, keep one, two questions each. He rarely lies. From round 2 or 3 his deck is marked, so he asks questions he already knows the answer to, to see whether you lie. The read is why he asked.
- Clues: TELL, his thumb along the edge. TABLE, nicks on high cards. MATH, 41 cards in a 40-card deck.
- Call: C, then MARKED DECK.
- Economy: seeing a raise made on a lie and winning +1 ŞANS (max 2). Win: Heat −2, his story, the Manager's Key. Lose: Heat +2.

### (c) THE CROOKED TABLE / HİLELİ MASA (find the cheat)

**Rules (20 seconds).** Tonight one of three tables is crooked. Play them and watch. When you're sure, call CHEAT at that table. Right: the House pays back double what you lost there. Wrong: the Clerk fines you and Heat goes up.

**Format.** A floor event in the wave 75 family. At Watch 1 the Compere names three tables, each with a readable brass UNDER WATCH plate; Tamsin runs the rig. A clue rings a soft bell and your hand jots it into the Tally Sheet (the journal page). To call, face the table and press C; the Clerk cuts in and the reveal plays. Clues use the duel channels:

| Channel | Examples |
|---|---|
| TELL | a still-one dealer blinks; Tamsin's eyes open; the Compere stutters |
| TABLE | a fourth cherry on a three-cherry reel; a ball that skips a pocket; a warm card |
| MATH | Tally Sheet: "0 cherries in 15 pulls, about 3 expected" |

**Beginner (N2).** One loud rig: the fourth cherry, the stutter, and a Tally line by the fifth pull.

**Hard (N6+).**
- **A decoy:** a fair table runs cold. The Sheet says "unlucky, not crooked: 1 in 6".
- **A quiet rig,** such as a fifth king in the shoe that shows only in the maths.
- **Ride the rig:** once you know its pattern (the crooked plinko pays every fifth ball to keep guests hooked), play it instead of calling. Every three rig plays: Heat +1. Calling is safe and riding is greedy: pillar 3's House face.

**Economy.** Right call: refund ×2 (capped at 25% of the quota), +1 ŞANS, Heat −1 (the Collector likes a guest the House owes). Wrong call: a fine of S and Heat +1.

## 6. Presentation: a duel as an anime moment

Built on `src/core/ui/manga` and `src/core/cutscene`. NEW marks what is missing.

1. **Sit.** Cut over your shoulder: the rival's silhouette across the felt. One Compere subtitle, then `manga.cutin` slides in the portrait and catchphrase.
2. **Seal.** The Clerk lays down the card and the stake is said aloud. Stamp SFX. `dialogue('clerk', …)` with typewriter and voice blips.
3. **Throws.** Your gloved hands act: RPS poses (NEW), the card peek, the locker pick. Each promise is typed onto a brass plate at the table edge (NEW) and into the Tally Sheet.
4. **Inner monologue (NEW).** Italic, bottom left, no speaker plate, 30 cps, at most 9 words. It explains people, never rules.
5. **Gambler's Eye (NEW).** Hold right mouse for 2 seconds of 25% time. The world goes grey; rims, eyes and glints keep their colour. Heartbeat, vignette, one inner line. Uses per duel: 1 + ŞANS ÷ 2, minimum 1. Calm Mode keeps only the slow motion and the line.
6. **Eye close-up (NEW camera key).** A hard cut to the rival's eyes (fov about 18) for 0.6 s on key throws. Mira's eyes turning white always gets one.
7. **Sting.** On a match-point break or a call: `manga.glyph('menace')` (GÜM GÜM GÜM), a brass sting, hitstop, and a flash unless reduceFlashing is on.
8. **Ruling.** The existing `reveal()`: freeze, Tally rewind, SAW, HAPPENED and WHY panels. The RULED stamp becomes the Clerk's.
9. **Last throw.** The music cuts. Only the clock and breathing.

**Sample, Lucan at match point:** he calls "Rock", cut to his wide eye. Inner line: "He's kept every word. That's the point." You hold the Eye and the watch glass shows your glove. Sting. C, MIRROR. The Clerk, the reveal, RULED.
