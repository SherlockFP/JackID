## What the screenshots showed (top issues, worst first)

1. **HUD top-center is a stacking bug.** The phase line (`MOLA / INTERMISSION …`), `NIGHT 1`,
   floor-event lines (`No lamps tonight…`), the warning chip (`PAWN WINDOW CLOSED ×2`), toasts
   (`DEPOSITED 1 TO THE BANK. P`) and the interact prompt (`E Play …`, `Bet 1-10`) render on top of
   each other and are unreadable. Evidence: `shots/run1/event-hud.png`.
2. **Toasts truncate mid-word** (`DEPOSITED 1 TO THE BANK. P…`). Same shot.
3. **Deadline counters contradict each other**: `QUOTA 16 / 24 · SHORT 8` and `DAWN 24/24` top-right
   vs `← 24m` bottom. A player cannot tell when the deadline is. Same shot.
4. **The goal is not at a glance**: quota/progress is buried among payout multipliers top-right.
5. **Night 1 is lost by the QA "sensible human" bot** (seed 1: bank 18 of quota 24 → mafia cutscene).
   Root cause found in the bet log: the bot deposited 9 of pocket 12 in Watch 1, then flat-bet the
   minimum (1) for the rest of the Night. The GAME is fine (sim bots reach N1 at 94–100 %,
   `node scripts/sim/run.mjs`), the harness bot's bankroll discipline is not.
   Full log: `shots/diag1.json`. This matters for testing: nothing past Night 1 can be screenshot-
   verified while the bot dies there.
6. **Starter draft**: readable and well designed, but surrounding text truncates (`+7 PULLS • NE…`,
   lore line cut at the bottom) and jargon is unexplained (`PAWN WINDOW`, `ENGINE • HOT`).
   Evidence: `shots/run1/run-start.png`, `shots/world/run-round1.png`.
7. **Big One**: the status band kept saying `REELS TURNING` over the finished result (state bug),
   and the reel symbols read as "white blobs" at size (MOTH/TOOTH/CANDLE) with no value-class cue.
   Evidence: `shots/bigone/bigone-f3200.png`, `shots/bigone/bigone-f900.png`.
8. **Dawn settle is near-black** (only the `BANK +6 INTEREST (55%)` line and the House box read),
   and the end-of-run / cutscene frames read as black voids at 1280x720.
   Evidence: `shots/nc2/nc-b.png`, `shots/nc2/nc-c.png`, `shots/run1/run-final.png`.
9. **Menu**: the "inside a jackpot" concept does not read (it looks like a casino floor in front of a
   cabinet), only `PLAY` is visible at first glance, sub-text is tiny. Evidence: `shots/menu/menu-round1.png`.

## Known cosmetic nits (post wave 1)

- **Nearby machine 3D screen text (the 'CRAT…' / 'BANK … NTEREST' artifact):** perspective-warped glyphs
  on a machine's own face/screen near the scratch vendor — `src/machines/scratch` paints no such text,
  so it is likely the Debt Terminal's BANK/interest row (`c_bank`/`c_interest`) rendered on its small
  canvas and cut at the screen edge. Machine-screen pass item: keep painted rows within canvas bounds.
- **Banner letter-drop frames:** `runBanner` (ui.ts `dropLetters`, 40 ms/letter) shows letters displaced
  mid-drop (a frame can read `BANK +13 NTEREST` with the `I` floating above the line). In motion it
  settles in under half a second and reads as juice; only screenshots catch it. If it must go: make the
  drop word-granular in `ui.ts` (a word appears at once) — deferred past this pass.
- Night Card preview pairs `Quota 36 · 27` — arrow format (`36 → 27`) landed in the dark-UI lane.
- Metres spacing (`24 m` not `24m`) fixed in `hud.ts` and `inGameState.ts` (was misread as minutes).
